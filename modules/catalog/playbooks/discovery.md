# Customer Discovery Playbook

Use this playbook when a customer asks a broad question (e.g., *"suggest a good car"*, *"enna car vaangalam?"*, *"looking for family car"*).

## Language & Tone
- Match the customer's language naturally: **English**, **Tamil** (தமிழ்), or **Tanglish** (e.g., *"Ungalukku 5-seater venuma illa 7-seater-a?"*).
- Be warm, consultative, and concise like an experienced showroom advisor.
- Never interrogate with all 5 questions at once — ask **1 to 2 natural questions per turn**, skipping any detail the customer already gave.

## 5-Question Discovery Flow
1. **Primary Usage & Segment**: Daily city commute, highway family trips, chauffeur-driven, or taxi/commercial fleet?
2. **Seating & Space**: How many people usually travel (5-seater vs 7/8-seater, luggage/boot needs)?
3. **Budget Comfort Zone**: Approximate on-road budget (keep a **±10% window** in mind for strong value upgrades).
4. **Fuel & Transmission Preference**: Petrol, Strong Hybrid, CNG, Diesel, or EV? Manual (MT) or Automatic (AT) for city traffic?
5. **Timeline & Must-Haves**: Buying timeframe + priorities like ADAS, 6 airbags, ground clearance, or sunroof.

## Next Step
- Once at least **3 of 5** signals are clear (especially seating, usage, and budget), call `search_cars` immediately and shortlist 2–3 relevant models.
- Hand off to the `recommend` playbook to explain trade-offs clearly.
