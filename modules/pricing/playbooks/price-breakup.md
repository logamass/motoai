# On-Road Price Breakup Playbook

Use this playbook whenever a customer asks for vehicle price, on-road cost, EMI/insurance breakup, or variant comparison.

## Non-Negotiable Grounding & Safety Rules
1. **Never generate or estimate prices from memory.** Always call `get_onroad_price` or `compare_variants` and quote the exact integer rupee figures returned.
2. **Never share bank account details.** Never mention account numbers, IFSC codes, UPI IDs, NEFT/RTGS instructions, or online transfer links.
3. **Mandatory Disclaimer & Effective Date**: Whenever quoting any price, always state the price list **effective date** and include:
   > *"Price prevailing at delivery applies. Always pay only at the showroom."*

## Explaining 1+3 vs 3+3 Insurance
- **1+3 Insurance** *(1 Year Own Damage + 3 Years Third Party)*: Lower upfront on-road price. From Year 2 onward, Own Damage (OD) cover is renewed annually at prevailing rates.
- **3+3 Insurance** *(3 Years Own Damage + 3 Years Third Party)*: Bundled 3-year OD protection upfront — locks in Year 1–3 OD coverage against annual premium inflation and saves renewal hassle.

## Explaining Optional Value Packs
- **Extended Warranty (EW)**: Protects expensive powertrain/hybrid/transmission components beyond standard warranty; strongly recommended for **AT, Hybrid, and high-mileage** buyers.
- **Service Pack / Accessories / Ceramic / Underbody**: Clarify which items are **optional dealer packages** versus mandatory statutory charges (**Ex-Showroom, TCS 1% on ₹10L+, RTO/Road Tax, and Insurance**).

## Language & Tone
- Match the customer's language (**English**, **Tamil**, or **Tanglish**) and format rupee figures clearly with Indian commas (e.g., `₹18,45,000`).
